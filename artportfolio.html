Artist Statement

"Faith is not confined to history; it is a living geometry."

My work investigates the intersection of ancient theological symbols and contemporary abstract expressionism. By stripping away figurative clutter, I seek to reveal the raw, elemental gravity of Christ's narrative—the tension between suffering and transcendence, the past agony and the present grace.

Using heavy textures, liturgical gold leaf, and muted earth tones juxtaposed with modern digital resonance, each piece serves as a silent sanctuary for meditation in an overly noisy world.
